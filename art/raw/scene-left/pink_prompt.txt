In this drawing, colour the satellite dish on the roof of the lab hot pink. Only that is coloured: flat hot pink (#FF0083) fills inside the existing outlines, with the dark outlines kept as they are and any light-grey shading on it becoming a slightly darker pink. Everything else in the drawing stays exactly as it is: the same lines, shapes, positions, line weight, pale background layer and framing, still in grey on pure white paper. Nothing else turns pink, nothing is added, removed or redrawn.
